About TDSHOP

TDSHOP is a demonstration online store where you can browse product prices, add items to your cart, and complete an order in just a few simple steps.

What You Can Do on This Website

  • view the catalog of items with updated prices;
  • add products to your shopping cart;
  • place an order with your delivery information;
  • track the status of your orders in the Orders section;
  • create a personal account and manage your profile information.

Payment Methods

  • cash on delivery (pay when picking up the parcel);
  • online payment by credit or debit card (Visa / MasterCard);
  • prepayment via bank details (available upon request).

The available payment method may vary depending on your location and the selected items.

Shipping & Returns

  • we deliver to your address or to the pickup point of your chosen delivery service;
  • delivery times depend on your region and the workload of the postal service;
  • if you receive a damaged product or an incorrect order, contact us within 14 days;
  • returns and exchanges are possible if the product is in its original condition and you have the receipt.

Support & Error Reports

If you encounter an error on the website, see incorrect pricing, cannot place an order, or have any questions — please contact our support email:

saywysss@gmail.com

When contacting support, please include your username (if you have one), the order number, and a short description of the issue — this will help us resolve it faster.

Important Notes

  • product prices and availability may change over time;
  • any personal data you provide is used only for order processing;
  • do not share your account information with third parties for security reasons.
